Table A3. PEV and Broward-Miami coral densities predicted for DRM2024.
Densities and PEV-minus-Broward-Miami differences are colonies/m2: posterior mean [pointwise 95% CrI].
Both regional means give each of NRC, IR, MR and OR weight 1/4 and set direction effects to zero.
All size classes, species sums and contrasts are calculated within each shared posterior draw before summarizing.
The adult total sums 17 modeled adult taxa. The full juvenile and All totals have no regional comparison.
Regional juvenile and All entries are blank where a juvenile component was not surveyed in the added broad DRM surveys.
Only MCAV, MEAN, MUSS and FAVI juveniles have complete added regional coverage; only MCAV has a survey-supported taxon-specific All row.
Surveyed juvenile groups (4) sums those four groups only, not the full juvenile community.
Pr_PEV_gt_Broward_Miami = Pr(PEV minus Broward-Miami >0 | data, model); Pr_PEV_lt_Broward_Miami = Pr(difference <0 | data, model).
Both columns report the observed fraction of paired posterior draws; they are not p-values and have no multiplicity adjustment.
No significance flags or posterior probability decision thresholds are applied. Intervals are pointwise, not simultaneous.
Probabilities are Monte Carlo estimates from 2000 pilot draws, displayed to four decimals (draw resolution 0.0005).
A displayed 0.0000 or 1.0000 means no opposite-sign draw was observed, not certainty about the population difference.
The model fit and all reported comparisons remain provisional pending final sampling.
